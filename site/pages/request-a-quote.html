Good to know

Before you send it

What’s the minimum corporate order?

Quotes start at 25 boxes. Logo pralines start at 100 pieces. For fewer, order directly from corporate gift boxes.

Can you deliver to every recipient?

Yes. Send us a spreadsheet of addresses and we ship each box separately with tracking.

How do you handle our logo?

Upload it with your quote or on upload your logo. We send a digital proof and print nothing until you approve it in writing.
