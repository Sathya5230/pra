# Ecommerce Trader Brief

## Mission
Build the commercial offer and trading logic behind the site.

## Commercial priorities
1. Gift boxes and ballotins as core revenue.
2. Premium gifts and seasonal drops as AOV drivers.
3. Vegan & Conscious as a growth route.
4. Corporate gifting as the highest-value lead funnel.
5. Delivery clarity as conversion protection.

## Launch assortment
Use `launch_assortment_matrix.csv`.

## Trading decisions required
- Final SKU list
- Variant structure
- Product family margins
- Pricing bands
- Gift message rules
- Shipping rules
- Warm weather shipping policy
- Corporate MOQ
- Sample pack offer
- Seasonal campaign calendar

## Product content required per SKU
Product format, piece count, net weight, chocolate type, filling type, flavour profile, ingredients, allergens, alcohol status, shelf life, storage advice, vegan/organic/Fairtrade claims, gift eligibility, corporate eligibility and delivery lead time.

## Shipping decisions required
Shipping zones, courier partners, cut-off times, blackout dates, heat threshold, cold pack logic, damaged product policy and replacement rules.
