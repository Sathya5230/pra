# Shopify UX Designer Brief

## Mission
Design a premium but practical Shopify experience for Belgian pralines, gifts, vegan/conscious buyers and corporate gifting.

## Primary UX principle
Guide, do not overwhelm.

## Top navigation
Shop  
Gifts  
Corporate Gifts  
Vegan & Conscious  
Our Chocolate  
Delivery  
About

## UX priorities
1. Buyer can find a gift in under 30 seconds.
2. Corporate buyer can request a quote without entering the retail checkout.
3. Vegan/conscious buyer can find claim-safe products immediately.
4. Delivery confidence appears before checkout.
5. Filters are collection-specific and restrained.

## Templates to design first
1. Homepage
2. Main collection page
3. Pralines & Truffles collection
4. Gift Boxes collection
5. Vegan & Conscious landing page
6. Corporate Gifting page
7. Pralines with Logo page
8. Product detail page
9. Request a Quote page
10. Delivery / Warm Weather page

## Required UX components
Header, mega menu, mobile menu, hero block, collection card, product card, filter drawer, badge system, trust strip, product tabs, gift message block, delivery estimate block, corporate CTA, quote form, logo upload module, FAQ accordion, testimonial block, cart drawer.
